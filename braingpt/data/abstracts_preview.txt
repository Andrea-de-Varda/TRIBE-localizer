We used fMRI to measure brain responses while healthy adult participants read short written items, presented one word at a time, and judged whether each item was correct. Each sentence described how one person treated another, followed by a description of their relationship that either fitted or did not fit the behaviour. For example, "Mohammed collects money from Carmen. Do you think that mohammed is Carmen's tenant or do you think that carmen is Mohammed's tenant? Carmen is Mohammed's tenant." (correct) versus "Mohammed collects money from Carmen. Do you think that mohammed is Carmen's tenant or do you think that carmen is Mohammed's tenant? Mohammed is Carmen's tenant." (incorrect). Items were presented in blocks, and responses were compared with a fixation baseline.
